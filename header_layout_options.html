🔍 Header Design Review & Layout Options

Comparing layout solutions to fix the awkward visual hierarchy and alignment in the current header.

Why the Screenshot Looks "Out of Order":
  • Triple Branding Overload: We have the Fifosys logo icon, followed by the pill badge that says "Fifosys BackupWatch Control Desk", followed by the main title that says "Fifosys BackupWatch". Repeating "Fifosys" 3 times in a 3-inch space makes it feel cluttered and unbalanced.
  • Awkward Asymmetrical Floating Pill: The pill badge is vertically floating above the title, creating an uneven top margin that makes the 48px square icon on the left look disconnected and vertically misaligned.
  • Divider Stature Mismatch: The vertical divider line was shorter than the combined height of the badge + title, breaking the visual container rule.
